# v0.1.0 - Complete Sets chapter reader

This is the first worthwhile, nonduplicative tranche of the ongoing Punjabi Shahmukhi OpenLogic edition.

Included reader coverage: OLP-0004 through OLP-0010, the complete Sets chapter (six sections, three original diagrams). The Naskh PDF has 12 pages; the Nastaliq PDF has 16 pages. Both were reproduced over three passes in the same recorded XeLaTeX toolchain, and every one of the 28 latest rendered pages was inspected. Section/figure direction and mixed RTL-prose/LTR-math defects found in an earlier build are fixed in these exact bytes.

Editable-source coverage is broader: OLP-0001 through OLP-0021 are translated and source-aligned reviewed (21 of 722 units). This includes the complete Relations chapter source and the opening Functions material, but only the Sets chapter has integrated reader/visual acceptance. The full edition, semantic HTML and treatment of the 80 nonordinary units remain in progress.

The release discloses independent machine translation, provisional specialist terminology, actual canon evidence and current QA. It includes a detailed terminology/source-fidelity review log with exact block locations, alternatives, uncertainty and questions for asynchronous expert feedback. Such feedback may inform later versions but is not a release hold.

The frozen English source revision is `9620cc73f9c8e0ad003c514a5d3748f29611c4c0`. Original work is by The Open Logic Project under CC BY 4.0. This translation/layout is modified and independent; no upstream endorsement is implied. Bundled fonts remain under OFL 1.1.
